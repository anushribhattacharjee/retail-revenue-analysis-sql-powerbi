# Retail Revenue Analysis (End-to-End Data Analytics Project)

## Project Overview
This project focuses on analyzing retail sales data to understand revenue performance, customer behavior, and discount impact.
The analysis was conducted end-to-end using SQL for data processing, Excel for validation, and Power BI for visualization.

---

## Business Objectives
- Analyze total revenue and revenue without discounts
- Understand the impact of discounts on overall revenue
- Identify top-performing cities, customers, and products
- Analyze monthly revenue trends
- Segment customers based on revenue contribution
- Identify inactive (churned) customers and associated revenue loss

---

## Tools & Technologies Used
- **MySQL** – Data storage, joins, and business analysis queries  
- **Excel** – Validation of SQL results using basic formulas  
- **Power BI** – Interactive dashboard and visual insights  
- **GitHub** – Version control and project documentation  

---

## Data Description
The dataset consists of three tables:
- **Customers**: Customer details, city, segment, and activity status  
- **Orders**: Transaction-level order data including quantity and discounts  
- **Products**: Product details and pricing information  

---

## Analysis Performed (SQL)
Key analyses performed using SQL include:
- Total revenue calculation (with and without discount)
- City-wise and customer-wise revenue analysis
- Monthly revenue trend analysis
- Customer segmentation into High, Mid, and Low value groups
- Identification of inactive customers and churned revenue

All SQL queries are documented in the `sql/` folder.

---

## Excel Validation
Excel was used to cross-check key metrics such as:
- Total revenue
- Revenue without discount
- Customer count
- Churned customers

This ensured consistency and accuracy before visualization.

---

## Power BI Dashboard
An interactive Power BI dashboard was created to present insights visually.
Key visuals include:
- KPI cards for revenue and discount impact
- City-wise and monthly revenue trends
- Customer segmentation and top customers
- Product-level revenue performance

Dashboard screenshots are available in the `screenshots/` folder, and the `.pbix` file is included in the `powerbi/` folder.

---

## Key Business Insights
- **Atta 5kg** is the highest revenue-generating product  
- **Wholesale customers** contribute the highest share of revenue  
- **June** recorded the highest monthly revenue  
- **Ankit Pal** is the top revenue-generating customer

This project demonstrates an end-to-end data analytics workflow, starting from raw data extraction to business insight generation.
It reflects strong analytical thinking, validation practices, and the ability to communicate insights through visualization.
